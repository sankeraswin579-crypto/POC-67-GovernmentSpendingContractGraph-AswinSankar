# Application Objective

The Government Spending Contract Graph is a full-stack intelligence platform designed to visualize government procurement and spending data through interactive relationship graphs, analytics dashboards, and decision-support features.

The application enables users to explore relationships between government agencies, contracts, and vendors while providing meaningful insights into spending patterns and procurement activities.

---

# Intended Users

The application is intended for:

- Government Procurement Officers
- Policy Analysts
- Financial Analysts
- Data Scientists
- Researchers
- Journalists
- Citizens interested in public spending transparency

---

# Issues Identified

The following minor observations were identified during the review:

- Graph rendering performance may decrease with larger datasets.
- Public datasets may contain incomplete or outdated records.
- Additional graph interactions such as node highlighting and advanced tooltips could further enhance usability.
- Export functionality is not available in the current implementation.

No critical usability or visualization issues were identified.

---

# Remaining Limitations

Current limitations include:

- Public government datasets may have incomplete metadata.
- Real-time synchronization with external government APIs is not implemented.
- Large datasets may impact graph visualization performance.
- AI-generated insights depend on the available contract information.

These limitations do not affect the core functionality of the application.
